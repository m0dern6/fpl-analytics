Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the FPL Analytics application handles information when you use our service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data.

FPL Analytics is designed entirely as a companion tool for Fantasy Premier League. The app only requires a public FPL Team ID to fetch public data directly from the official Fantasy Premier League APIs. We do not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or submit any personally identifiable information (PII).

2. Local Storage

Any preferences or identifiers (such as your saved FPL Team ID or app theme preferences) are stored strictly locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers or any third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

The app communicates with the official Fantasy Premier League APIs to retrieve football statistics, gameweek points, and team data. This connection is necessary for the app to function. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising SDKs, or background data harvesting tools.

4. Data Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your data on any external servers, there is no personal data for us to delete. If you wish to remove your locally saved data, you can simply clear the app's data in your device settings or uninstall the app entirely.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect contact information, any updates to this policy will simply be reflected on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact the developer directly.
